# Product Features & Functional Guide

## 1. Kiosk Check-In System
The Kiosk is the primary point of entry for children.
- **Identification**: Supports Phone/PIN, QR Code scanning, and NFC.
- **Hardware Binding**: Kiosks must be enrolled and active to perform transactions.
- **Wellness Check**: Optional health screening before check-in.
- **Label Printing**: Automatic separation of Child Tags and Parent Claim Tickets.

## 2. Attendance Management
Real-time dashboard for administrators and staff.
- **Live Feed**: Real-time websocket updates of all arrivals/departures.
- **Manual Overrides**: Signed-out by authorized staff when physical tickets are lost.
- **Class Distribution**: Heatmap and counters of occupancy across classrooms.

## 3. Church & CRM (Guest Journey)
Designed for organizational growth and pastoral care.
- **Member Roster**: Complete database of all families and individuals.
- **Guest Tracking**: Follow-up workflow for first-time visitors.
- **Interaction Logs**: Record of pastoral care, notes, and milestones.

## 4. Staff & Team Coordination
- **Shift Scheduling**: Roster management for teachers and volunteers.
- **Staff PINs**: Dedicated secure codes for staff to unlock kiosk tools.
- **Certification Tracking**: (In Progress) Management of background checks and training.

## 5. Attendance Rewards
Gamification system to encourage consistent attendance.
- **Point Accrual**: Automated point assignment on check-in.
- **Redemption**: Reward catalog where children can exchange points for prizes.
- **History**: Full ledger of point transactions.

## 6. Messaging & Communications
- **Broadcasts**: Send mass alerts to all parents or specific classes.
- **Direct Messaging**: One-on-one communication between staff and parents.
- **Push Notifications**: Integrated with Edge Functions for mobile alerts.

## 7. Forensic & Audit Dashboard
- **Activity Logs**: Every button click and data change recorded.
- **Access Logs**: Tracking "Who viewed what" for sensitive medical data.
- **Report Sealing**: Cryptographic verification of attendance exports.
